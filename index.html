Roof Year Built Underwriting Validator

Underwriting Rule: Without Year Built ➔ Blank • Roof Year is NEVER less than Year Built, but it can equal (Roof Year ≥ Year Built)
Core Validation Rules Strict Underwriting
• Multi-Year Roof (e.g. 2005/2006): Pick GREATER value (2006) and check Year Built not greater than Roof Year
• Year Comparison: Roof Year is never less than Year Built, but it can equal (e.g. YB: 2005, Roof: 2000 ➔ BLANK; Roof ≥ 2005 ➔ VALID)
• Without Year Built: Roof Year cannot be accurate ➔ BLANK
• Missing Roof Year: When Year Built given but Roof Year missing ➔ BLANK
• Valid Range: 1753 ≤ Year ≤ 2026 (Current Year)
Example Rule Outcomes
YB: 2005, Roof: 2005/2006 ➔ 2006 (Picks greater 2006, 2006 ≥ 2005)
YB: 2008, Roof: 2005/2006 ➔ [BLANK] (Picks 2006, but YB 2008 > 2006)
YB: 2005, Roof: 2005 ➔ 2005 (Valid, Equal / Original Roof)
YB: 2005, Roof: 2010 ➔ 2010 (Valid, Re-roofed: 2010 ≥ 2005)
YB: 2005, Roof: 2000 ➔ [BLANK] (Never less than Year Built!)
YB: 2005, Roof: [Blank] ➔ [BLANK] (Roof Year Missing)
YB: [Blank], Roof: 2005 ➔ [BLANK] (Without Year Built)

No of Stores Underwriting Engine

Underwriting Rule: Negative Values ➔ Blank (-5➔Blank) • Always Whole No (3.5➔4, 4.2➔5) • Ranges/Multi-values Pick Max (2 & 3➔3, 1,2➔2, 2/3➔3) • Non/Blank➔Blank
Core Normalization Rules Strict Underwriting
• Negative Values & Zero: If there are negative values, leave blank (e.g. -5 ➔ blank, -2 ➔ blank). Stories cannot be negative in underwriting. Zero (0) also leaves blank.
• Always Whole Number (Round UP): Any decimal rounds up to the next integer (3.5 ➔ 4, 4.2 ➔ 5, 1.1 ➔ 2).
• Ranges & Multiple Numbers: Always pick the higher / maximum number (2 & 3 ➔ 3, 1,2 ➔ 2, 2/3 ➔ 3, 2-4 ➔ 4).
• Blanks & "non": Empty lines, whitespace, non, none, n/a, and 0 leave blank.

non / none ➔ [BLANK] (Non-applicable)
